PROCUREMENT POLICY
Learning Sample - Version 1.0

PURPOSE

This policy defines the learning workflow for procurement
inside the Inventory Agent demonstration system.

PURCHASE REQUESTS

1. A reorder recommendation does not constitute approval.

2. A purchase request must first be created as DRAFT.

3. A DRAFT purchase request does not mean that an order has
   been placed with a supplier.

4. Purchase requests must be reviewed by an authorized
   approver before procurement can proceed.

5. Approved supplier records must be used when preparing
   purchase requests.

6. Supplier names, pricing, minimum order quantities and
   lead times must come from approved supplier records.

EMERGENCY PROCUREMENT

7. Emergency procurement must be escalated to a procurement
   manager or other authorized approver.

8. The reason for emergency procurement must be documented.

9. Emergency procurement must not bypass audit requirements.

SUPPLIER SELECTION

10. A uniquely preferred approved supplier may be selected
    according to the configured supplier record.

11. When multiple suppliers are available and no supplier is
    uniquely preferred, a human procurement decision is required.

12. The AI must not invent suppliers, prices or supplier terms.

APPROVAL

13. Only an authorized approval workflow may change a purchase
    request to APPROVED.

14. The Inventory Agent must not claim that a purchase was
    approved unless the approval system confirms it.
