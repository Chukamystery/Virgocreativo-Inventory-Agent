WAREHOUSE RECEIVING SOP
Learning Sample - Version 1.0

PURPOSE

This SOP defines the learning procedure for receiving goods
into the warehouse.

STANDARD RECEIVING PROCEDURE

1. Confirm the delivery reference or purchase documentation.

2. Verify the product identity and quantity delivered.

3. Inspect the goods for visible damage.

4. Compare the delivered quantity with the expected quantity.

5. Record accepted stock through the approved RECEIPT stock
   movement workflow.

6. Inventory must not be updated by directly editing the
   quantity_on_hand field.

DAMAGED GOODS

7. Damaged goods must be separated from accepted stock.

8. Do not record damaged goods as normal available inventory
   unless they have been formally accepted.

9. Record the damage details and delivery reference.

10. Notify the responsible warehouse or procurement person.

11. Follow the organization's return, replacement or claims
    process where applicable.

QUANTITY DIFFERENCES

12. If the quantity delivered differs from the expected
    quantity, document the discrepancy.

13. Only the verified accepted quantity should be recorded
    through the approved stock-movement workflow.

14. Significant discrepancies should be escalated to the
    responsible manager.

AUDIT

15. All inventory receipts must remain traceable through stock
    movement and audit records.
